Nature before the heat

Start early, move slowly and let the habitats wake up around you.

The Gambia is one of West Africa's most rewarding places for accessible birdwatching. Around Kotu and nearby wetland paths, you can enjoy color, calls, movement and photography without needing a demanding expedition.

This private tour is beginner-friendly, but it can also be adapted for guests who want more time with binoculars, a quieter pace and specific habitats.

Suggested flow

A gentle morning for observation, photos and quiet paths.

The exact route depends on pickup area, weather, season and guest pace.

Stop 1

Early pickup

Wetlands and observation points
