When does The Gambia’s tourist season start?

November is a useful starting point for planning a winter visit. The main winter tourism window is usually November to April. The Government of The Gambia describes the wider dry season as November to May, with the rainy season generally running from June to October. These are typical seasonal patterns, rather than a weather forecast for your travel dates.

For the coming season, that gives you a clear planning window: November 2026 through April 2027, with May worth discussing if you prefer a later trip. Conditions can differ between the coast and the interior, so we plan the route and pace around where you want to go.

Make a little room beyond the hotel

You do not need to fill every day. A couple of well-chosen excursions can give a beach holiday a completely different feel.

  • For a first introduction: the Bijilo, Kachikally and Serrekunda route brings together forest wildlife, local traditions, a market and an artist’s studio.
  • For slower travel: choose a River Gambia cruise and fishing day. There is time to try fishing, enjoy lunch or simply watch the riverbanks pass by.
  • For forest and mangrove scenery: explore Makasutu Forest, or ask about a more active kayaking outing.
  • For history that stays with you: visit Kunta Kinteh Island, Albreda and Juffureh. Choose the ferry-based route or discuss the premium boat option.
  • For coast and local life: combine a river outing, Sanyang and the fishing coast on our South Gambia day trip.

Some shared departures have a regular day: Makasutu is advertised on Sundays and the Banjul and Serrekunda markets route on Thursdays. We confirm the departure and group arrangements before you book. Private dates are also worth asking about.

Have more time? Go a little further.

A longer journey gives you room for landscapes beyond the coast. Our two-day Janjanbureh route combines river scenery, an overnight stay and Wassu. For Senegal wildlife and mangroves, consider the Fathala and Saloum Delta journey.

The eight-day Gambia and Senegal itinerary reaches Dakar, Gorée Island, Bandia and the Saloum Delta, before returning to The Gambia. It joins our existing seven- and ten-day routes, giving you several ways to build a longer visit. Hotel choices, border logistics and the final programme are confirmed for your dates.

Visiting aboard a cruise ship? Our shore excursions are planned separately around your arrival, all-aboard time and available hours. Send your ship details so we can recommend a realistic day.
