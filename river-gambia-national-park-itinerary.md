## **WILDLIFE EXPECTATIONS & SEASONAL GUIDE**

### **Guaranteed Encounters:**
🦛 **Hippos:** 95% guarantee (large resident populations)
🐊 **Nile Crocodiles:** 90% guarantee (excellent viewing opportunities)
🦅 **Birds:** 100% guarantee (280+ species, always active)
🐵 **Primates:** 85% guarantee (vervets, baboons, possible chimpanzees)
🦎 **Monitor Lizards:** 90% guarantee (active throughout day)

### **Seasonal Wildlife Activity:**

**Peak Dry Season (December-February):**
- Optimal wildlife viewing conditions
- Animals concentrated around water sources
- Clear visibility and excellent photography
- Comfortable temperatures for full-day activities

**Early Dry Season (November, March):**
- Good wildlife viewing with fewer crowds
- Migratory birds arriving/departing
- Pleasant temperatures and clear skies
- Excellent value with high-season quality

**Wet Season (June-October):**
- Lush green landscapes and dramatic scenery
- Baby animals and breeding season activity
- Fewer tourists and more intimate experiences
- Some areas may be inaccessible due to flooding
