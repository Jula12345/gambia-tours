A closer look at the journey

A day worth getting out for.

The final order and timings are confirmed for your departure.

01

Kachikally and Bakau

Visit the crocodile pool and discover the traditions surrounding this important local site.

02

Lamin Lodge and the mangroves

Take a boat trip through river and mangrove landscapes, with the Daranka area and its distinctive elephant-shaped tree on the route.

03

Sanyang Paradise Beach

Enjoy time by the Atlantic and an included Gambian-style lunch. Beach activities depend on local conditions.

04

Tanji fishing village

See the working fishing coast and colorful boats before returning to your hotel. Boat arrivals and the light vary with the day.
