10-Day Senegal & Gambia Discovery

A private West Africa route for travelers who want Dakar, Lac Rose, Fathala safari, Banjul, UNESCO heritage, the River Gambia and local culture in one smooth itinerary.

Signature multi-day route

Senegal first, then five focused days in The Gambia.

This itinerary starts with Dakar and Lac Rose, continues to Fathala Wildlife Reserve, then crosses into The Gambia for Banjul, Kunta Kinteh Island, Abuko, Kachikally, Tanji, Janjanbureh, the River Gambia and Wassu Stone Circles.

It is designed for small groups, couples and travelers who want a comfortable pace, private support and a Polish-speaking guide option without losing the feeling of a real West Africa journey.

Day-by-day plan

A complete route without rushing the border crossings or river days.

The final order can be adjusted to flight times, local conditions and your preferred pace before confirmation.

Day 1

Dakar arrival and city orientation

Meet your guide at Blaise Diagne International Airport, transfer to the coast and begin with Dakar orientation: Sandaga Market area, African Renaissance Monument and Independence Square when timing allows.

DSS airport to Dakar: about 45 km.
Day 2

Lac Rose and Dakar craft stops

Visit Lac Rose, learn about salt harvesting and return toward Dakar for craft markets, city viewpoints or a relaxed afternoon by the ocean.

Dakar to Lac Rose and back: about 70 km total.
Day 3

Fathala Wildlife Reserve safari

Travel south-east through Senegal countryside for a safari day at Fathala, with 4x4 wildlife viewing and an overnight lodge atmosphere near the border route.

Dakar to Fathala: about 190 km.
Day 4

Cross into The Gambia and explore Banjul

Assisted Senegal-Gambia border crossing, then continue toward Banjul for Arch 22, the National Museum area, Albert Market and a first taste of Gambian coastal life.

Fathala to Banjul route: about 120 km including border time.
Day 5

Kunta Kinteh Island, Albreda, Juffureh and river time

A full heritage day around Kunta Kinteh Island, local villages and the River Gambia, with historical context and optional boat time depending on conditions.

Coast to river heritage route: about 80 km plus boat transfers.
Day 6

Abuko, Kachikally and Tanji fishing culture

Nature and culture day with Abuko Nature Reserve, Kachikally Crocodile Pool and Tanji Fish Market, one of the most vivid coastal scenes in The Gambia.

Coastal circuit: about 60 km total.
Day 7

Janjanbureh and River Gambia boat safari

Drive inland toward Janjanbureh for a river-focused day with boat safari, wildlife watching, village life and an overnight stay in a riverside lodge atmosphere.

Coast to Janjanbureh: about 200 km inland.
Day 8

Wassu Stone Circles and return to the Atlantic coast

Visit the Wassu Stone Circles and return toward the Atlantic coast with scenic stops, photography breaks and a softer beachside evening.

Janjanbureh to coast: about 200 km with stops.
Day 9

Return to Senegal and Dakar farewell evening

Cross back into Senegal with assistance, continue to Dakar and leave time for craft shopping, textiles, wood carvings or a farewell dinner plan.

Gambia to Dakar: about 350 km including border time.
Day 10

Dakar airport transfer

Final breakfast, last stops if your flight time allows, and private transfer to Blaise Diagne International Airport for departure.

Dakar to DSS airport: about 45 km.
