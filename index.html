Gambia + Senegal Casamance from $980 per person

7-Day Gambia & Casamance Discovery

A warm cultural route from the Gambian coast into Senegal's Casamance region, designed for travelers who want villages, river landscapes, markets and relaxed overland travel.

  1. Day 1: Airport pickup, coastal hotel check-in and welcome briefing in Kololi or Kotu.
  2. Day 2: Banjul city tour, Albert Market, Arch 22 area and local craft stops.
  3. Day 3: Tanji fishing coast, Sanyang beach and local village life.
  4. Day 4: Cross into Senegal's Casamance, Ziguinchor route and overnight stay.
  5. Day 5: Casamance villages, craft culture, river scenery and optional boat time.
  6. Day 6: Return toward The Gambia with stops shaped around markets, nature and photography.
  7. Day 7: Flexible coastal morning, souvenir stop and airport or hotel transfer.

Not included

International flights, meals, drinks, personal expenses, travel insurance and tips.

7-Day Senegambia Wildlife & Culture Loop

A richer cross-border route for travelers who want Gambia highlights plus Senegal wildlife, river landscapes and a stronger sense of the Senegambia region.

  1. Day 1: Arrival transfer, coastal hotel check-in and private route briefing.
  2. Day 2: Kotu wetlands, Bakau, local markets and sunset along the coast.
  3. Day 3: Banjul, ferry logistics and north bank route toward Senegal.
  4. Day 4: Senegal wildlife or nature reserve day, with flexible timing for photos.
  5. Day 5: Senegal village culture, crafts and river or mangrove landscapes.
  6. Day 6: Return to The Gambia with stops for local life, viewpoints and shopping.
  7. Day 7: Relaxed final morning, custom stop and airport or hotel transfer.

10-Day Senegal & Gambia Discovery

A complete West Africa route starting and ending in Dakar, with Senegal highlights, Fathala safari, five days in The Gambia, UNESCO heritage and the River Gambia.

  1. Days 1-2: Dakar arrival, city orientation, African Renaissance Monument, markets and Lac Rose.
  2. Day 3: Fathala Wildlife Reserve safari and overnight lodge stay.
  3. Day 4: Assisted border crossing into The Gambia and Banjul city tour.
  4. Days 5-6: Kunta Kinteh Island, river boat time, Abuko, Kachikally and Tanji fishing culture.
  5. Days 7-8: Janjanbureh, River Gambia boat safari, village life and Wassu Stone Circles.
  6. Days 9-10: Return to Dakar, craft shopping, farewell evening and airport transfer.

Gambia tour FAQ

Questions travelers ask before booking

Do you offer private tours in Gambia?

Yes. Most tours can be private, with pickup from the main coastal hotel areas and a flexible route.

Can I book a Polish-speaking guide in Gambia?

Yes, Polish-speaking guides are available on request. It is best to mention Polish language preference when sending the booking request.

Where do tours start?

Tours can start from Kololi, Kotu, Senegambia, Bijilo, Cape Point, Banjul and nearby hotel areas.

Can you build a custom Gambia itinerary?

Yes. Share your interests, hotel location, date and group size, and we will suggest a private route.

Do you offer multi-day Gambia and Senegal tours?

Yes. We offer private 7-day and 10-day itineraries combining The Gambia with Senegal routes such as Casamance, Dakar, Fathala, river landscapes and cultural visits.

What is included in the multi-day tours?

Private guide/driver, vehicle, fuel, accommodation, airport transfers, border assistance, listed visits and route planning are included. International flights and some meals/drinks are not included unless confirmed in your quote.
