7-Day Gambia & Casamance Discovery

A warm cultural route from the Gambian coast into Senegal's Casamance region, designed for travelers who want villages, river landscapes, markets and relaxed overland travel.

  1. Day 1: Airport pickup, coastal hotel check-in and welcome briefing in Kololi or Kotu.

  6. Day 6: Return toward The Gambia with stops shaped around markets, nature and photography.

Gambia tour FAQ

Questions travelers ask before booking

Do you offer private tours in Gambia?

Yes. Most tours can be private, with pickup from the main coastal hotel areas and a flexible route.

Which languages do your guides speak?

Our tours are led in English. Polish-speaking guides are also available by prior arrangement. Please confirm your preferred guiding language when you enquire.

Where do tours start?

Tours can start from Kololi, Kotu, Senegambia, Bijilo, Cape Point, Banjul and nearby hotel areas.

Can you build a custom Gambia itinerary?

Yes. Share your interests, hotel location, date and group size, and we will suggest a private route.

Do you offer multi-day Gambia and Senegal tours?

Yes. Our collection includes two-day journeys and seven-, eight- and ten-day Gambia and Senegal itineraries. Each route has its own programme and booking conditions.

What is included in the multi-day tours?

Inclusions differ by route. See the itinerary for transport, guiding, accommodation, meals and activities, and review your personal quote before booking. International flights are excluded unless explicitly agreed.
