## 🔧 RESEARCH TOOLS & TECHNIQUES

### **Website Research Method:**
1. **Primary Navigation:** Look for "Contact," "About," "Trade," "Professionals"
2. **Team/Staff Pages:** Find product managers, partnership roles
3. **Press Releases:** Often contain management names and contacts
4. **Footer Information:** Sometimes contains department-specific emails

### **LinkedIn Research Strategy:**
```
Search Terms to Use:
- "[Company Name] Partnership Manager"  
- "[Company Name] Product Manager Africa"
- "[Company Name] Business Development"
- "[Company Name] Travel Trade"

Profile Research:
- Current job title and responsibilities
- Direct contact information if available  
- Connection opportunities
- Mutual connections for warm introductions
```

### **Phone Research Approach:**
```
When Calling:
1. "Hello, I'm looking to speak with someone about travel partnerships"
2. "We're a ground operator in West Africa looking to connect with your Africa product team"
3. "Could you direct me to your partnerships or product development department?"
4. Ask for direct email addresses and best contact times
```

### **Email Format Prediction:**
```
Common Patterns:
- firstname.lastname@company.com
- firstname@company.com  
- department@company.com (partnerships, africa, product)
- info@company.com (backup general contact)
```
