## Photo Optimization Checklist

### **Visual Elements**
☑️ **Clear subject focus** - Guest interaction prominently featured
☑️ **Good lighting** - Natural daylight preferred, avoid harsh shadows
☑️ **Action shots** - Capture the moment of interaction/discovery
☑️ **Genuine emotions** - Surprise, joy, wonder, accomplishment
☑️ **Cultural context** - Include local guides, traditional settings
☑️ **Safety visible** - Show professional equipment, guide supervision

### **Composition Tips**
☑️ **Rule of thirds** - Place subject off-center for dynamic feel
☑️ **Leading lines** - Use natural elements to guide eye to action
☑️ **Depth of field** - Blur background to focus on main subject
☑️ **Multiple angles** - Wide shots for context, close-ups for emotion

## A/B Testing Strategy

### **Test Variables**
1. **Hook style:** Question vs Statement vs Emoji
2. **Length:** Short (50 words) vs Medium (100 words) vs Long (150+ words)
3. **Focus:** Adventure vs Culture vs Safety vs Photography
4. **CTA placement:** Beginning vs Middle vs End

### **Success Metrics**
- Click-through rate from photo to booking page
- Time spent on listing page
- Conversion rate (views to bookings)
- Engagement (likes, saves, shares)
