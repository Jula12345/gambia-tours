## **GAMBIA BIRDING PARADISE DAY TRIP**
**Duration:** Full Day (9 hours) | **Price:** €110 per person | **Group:** Max 6 guests

### **TOUR OVERVIEW:**
Explore Gambia's incredible avian diversity with expert ornithologists. Visit multiple habitats from mangroves to savanna, spotting endemic species and spectacular migrants. Perfect for serious birders and photography enthusiasts.

---

### **DETAILED ITINERARY:**

**6:00 AM - Early Morning Pickup**
- Professional ornithologist guide pickup
- Pre-dawn departure for optimal bird activity
- Equipment check: binoculars, field guides, spotting scope
- Coffee and light breakfast pack provided

**6:30 AM - Abuko Nature Reserve (Forest Birding)**
- Gambia's premier birding location
- Forest species: Green Turaco, Ahanta Francolin
- Riverine birds: Giant Kingfisher, White-backed Night Heron
- Endemic subspecies and rare forest specialists
- Professional photography guidance

**9:00 AM - Lamin Lodge (Mangrove Ecosystem)**
- Boardwalk through pristine mangroves
- Mangrove specialties: Mangrove Sunbird, Green-backed Twinspot
- Waders and shorebirds at low tide
- Traditional breakfast with birding views
- Expert identification help with challenging species

**11:30 AM - Kotu Creek & Sewage Ponds**
- Premier shorebird and wader habitat
- Seasonal migrants: Whimbrel, Curlew Sandpiper
- Terns, gulls, and coastal species
- Photography from purpose-built hides
- Species counting and listing

**1:00 PM - Birding Lunch Break**
- Lunch at birding-friendly restaurant
- Continued bird observation during meal
- Review morning's sightings and photos
- Afternoon habitat planning

**2:30 PM - Tanji Fish Market & Beach**
- Seabirds and coastal species
- Brown-necked Raven, Western Reef Heron
- Traditional fishing boats with bird life
- Beach birding and photography
- Cultural interaction with local fishermen

**4:00 PM - Bijilo Forest Park (Dry Forest)**
- Dry woodland species
- Violet Turaco, Yellow-crowned Gonolek
- Monkey troops with associated bird species
- Sunset birding opportunities
- Final species count and review

**5:30 PM - Return Journey**
- Species list compilation (target: 100+ species)
- Photography review and sharing
- Bird identification confirmation
- Planning return visits for different seasons

**6:30 PM - Return to Hotel**

## **BIRD SPECIES HIGHLIGHTS:**

### **Endemic & Near-Endemic Species:**
- **Gambian subspecies** of many West African birds
- **Ahanta Francolin** - Forest ground bird
- **Green-winged Pytilia** - Spectacular finch
- **Red-billed Firefinch** - Common but beautiful

### **Spectacular Species to Expect:**
🦅 **Raptors:** Tawny Eagle, Dark Chanting Goshawk, Hooded Vulture
🦜 **Colorful Species:** Violet Turaco, Green Turaco, Little Bee-eater
🐦 **Kingfishers:** Pied, Giant, Malachite, Blue-breasted
🕊️ **Ground Birds:** Stone Partridge, Double-spurred Francolin
🦆 **Waterbirds:** African Jacana, Purple Heron, Goliath Heron

### **Seasonal Specialties:**
**November-March (Peak Season):**
- Palearctic migrants: European Bee-eater, Yellow Wagtail
- Perfect weather for all-day birding
- Highest species diversity

**April-October (Resident Focus):**
- Breeding plumage residents
- Fewer crowds, more intimate experience
- Lush landscapes and active nesting

## **SEASONAL BIRDING CALENDAR:**

### **Peak Season (November-February):**
- **Best overall birding** with migrants and residents
- **Perfect weather** - cool mornings, comfortable temperatures
- **Highest species count** potential (120+ species possible)
- **Palearctic migrants** at peak numbers

### **Breeding Season (March-June):**
- **Active breeding behavior** and displays
- **Best photography** opportunities with breeding plumage
- **Nest finding** opportunities with expert guide
- **Territorial singing** makes identification easier

### **Wet Season (July-October):**
- **Lush landscapes** and active insect life
- **Fewer birders** for more exclusive experience
- **Local breeding species** at peak activity
- **Different habitat accessibility** due to rains
