## **💰 INSURANCE COST BREAKDOWN**

### **Annual Premium Estimates:**
- **€2M Public Liability:** €1,500-2,500
- **€1M Professional Indemnity:** €800-1,500  
- **Legal Expenses:** €300-500
- **Product Liability:** €400-600
- **Equipment Coverage:** €200-400

**Total Annual Cost:** €3,200-5,500

### **Payment Options:**
- **Annual payment:** Usually 5-10% discount
- **Monthly payments:** Available with most insurers
- **Quarterly payments:** Good compromise option

### **Cost-Saving Tips:**
- **Higher deductibles** reduce premiums
- **Safety training certification** can lower costs
- **Multiple year agreements** often get discounts
- **Industry association membership** may qualify for group rates
