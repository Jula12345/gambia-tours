Make your hours ashore count

A little time in port. A whole new perspective.

With years of experience organizing day trips in The Gambia, our team helps cruise visitors make the most of their time on land. Choose a city discovery, a nature excursion or a boat trip, or ask us to combine a few highlights into a private day.

We plan a realistic route using your scheduled arrival and all-aboard time, allowing time for meeting your guide and the return journey. Your meeting point, transport, return target and full price are agreed before you book.
