## **TOUR OVERVIEW DESCRIPTION:**

Discover authentic Gambia through this comprehensive 7-day cultural immersion and wildlife conservation experience. Explore traditional Mandinka villages, observe diverse wildlife in national parks, learn about conservation efforts, and engage with local communities. This carefully designed program combines cultural education, wildlife observation, and sustainable tourism practices.

**Perfect for travelers seeking authentic African experiences, wildlife enthusiasts, cultural explorers, and conscious tourists interested in conservation and community-based tourism.**
