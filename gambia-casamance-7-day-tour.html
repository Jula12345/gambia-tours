Casamance route

A compact cross-border itinerary with culture, coast and river atmosphere.

This tour is built for travelers who want more than a single day trip, but still prefer a route that feels warm and manageable. It combines the Gambian coast, Banjul, local markets, fishing villages and Senegal's Casamance region.

The package includes private guide/driver, vehicle, fuel, accommodation, airport transfers, border assistance, listed visits and route planning. International flights, meals, drinks, personal expenses, insurance and tips are not included unless confirmed in your quote.

Day-by-day plan

Seven days across The Gambia and Senegal's Casamance region.

The exact hotels and order are confirmed around dates, border timing and room availability.

Day 1

Arrival, coastal check-in and route briefing

Airport pickup, transfer to the coastal area and a relaxed welcome briefing in Kololi or Kotu.

Private arrival transfer included.
Day 2

Banjul city, Albert Market and craft stops

Visit Banjul, Albert Market, the Arch 22 area and selected craft or heritage stops.

Good day for culture and local life.
Day 3

Tanji coast, Sanyang and village life

Explore the fishing coast, Sanyang beach atmosphere and selected local village experiences.

Coast and community focus.
Day 4

Cross into Senegal's Casamance

Assisted border crossing, overland route into Casamance and overnight stay around the Ziguinchor route.

Border timing can vary by day.
Day 5

Casamance villages, crafts and river scenery

Spend the day with village culture, craft stops, green landscapes and optional boat time when available.

Casamance is known for a softer, greener rhythm.
Day 6

Return toward The Gambia

Travel back with stops shaped around markets, nature, photography and local life.

Flexible return day.
Day 7

Final coastal morning and transfer

Relaxed final morning, souvenir stop if time allows and airport or hotel transfer.

Timing depends on flight or onward plan.
