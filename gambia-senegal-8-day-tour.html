A closer look at the journey

Your journey, step by step.

The final order and timings are confirmed for your departure.

Day 1

Arrival in The Gambia

Airport transfer to your coastal accommodation and time to settle in. The final hotel is confirmed with your booking.

Day 2

Banjul and Gambian culture

Explore Banjul and its cultural stops, with Kachikally and a visit to Monkey Park on the proposed route.

Day 3

Into Senegal: Lac Rose and Bandia

Travel to Senegal, visit the Lac Rose area and enjoy a Bandia reserve safari before continuing to Dakar. The lake’s appearance varies naturally.

Day 4

Dakar and Gorée Island

Discover Dakar, including the African Renaissance Monument, and take a boat to Gorée for an important encounter with the region’s history.

Day 5

The Saloum Delta

Head south for a boat trip through Saloum’s mangroves before returning to The Gambia.

Day 6

Kunta Kinteh Island

Spend the day on the River Gambia and visit Kunta Kinteh Island, with time to reflect on the island’s history.

Day 7

Serrekunda and the coast

Explore the market and local communities, then enjoy time at Paradise Beach.

Day 8

Departure

Breakfast and an airport transfer arranged around your flight schedule.
