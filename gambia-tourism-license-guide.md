## **📋 REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION FOR LICENSE APPLICATION**

### **1. Business Registration Documents:**
- Certificate of Incorporation (company registration)
- Business Registration Certificate from Registrar General
- Tax Clearance Certificate from GRA (Gambia Revenue Authority)
- Valid Tax Identification Number (TIN)

### **2. Tourism-Specific Requirements:**
- Detailed business plan for tourism operations
- List of proposed tourism services and activities
- Qualified staff credentials and certifications
- Safety and emergency procedures documentation
- Insurance coverage proof (when obtained)

### **3. Financial Requirements:**
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Proof of minimum capital requirements
- Audited financial statements (if applicable)
- Payment of license fees

### **4. Operational Standards:**
- Office premises registration and lease agreement
- Vehicle registration and insurance (for transport services)
- Boat registration and safety certificates (for water activities)
- Guide training certificates and qualifications
- First Aid and safety training documentation

## **💰 LICENSE FEES AND COSTS**

### **Estimated Costs:**
- **Tourism Operator License:** D15,000 - D25,000 ($225-375 USD)
- **Annual Renewal Fee:** D8,000 - D12,000 ($120-180 USD)
- **Guide Registration:** D2,000 per guide ($30 USD)
- **Vehicle Registration:** D5,000 per vehicle ($75 USD)
- **Processing Fees:** D3,000 ($45 USD)

**Total Initial Cost:** ~D30,000-40,000 ($450-600 USD)

## **📝 STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION PROCESS**

### **Step 1: Initial Consultation**
- Contact GTB licensing department
- Schedule appointment to discuss requirements
- Obtain official application forms
- Get checklist of required documents

### **Step 2: Business Registration (if not already done)**
- Register company with Registrar General
- Obtain Tax Identification Number
- Open business bank account
- Get tax clearance certificate

### **Step 3: Prepare Tourism Business Plan**
**Include:**
- Company background and ownership
- Detailed service offerings (7-day tours, day trips, etc.)
- Target markets (Polish tourists, European markets)
- Safety protocols and procedures
- Staff qualifications and training plans
- Financial projections and sustainability

### **Step 4: Submit Application Package**
- Complete all application forms
- Attach all required documents
- Pay application fees
- Submit to GTB licensing department

### **Step 5: Inspection and Compliance**
- Schedule premises inspection (if required)
- Demonstrate compliance with safety standards
- Present staff qualifications
- Show operational procedures

### **Step 6: Final Approval and License Issuance**
- Receive approval notification
- Pay final license fees
- Collect official license certificate
- Register in GTB tourism operator database

## **🛡️ INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS FOR LICENSE**

### **Mandatory Insurance Coverage:**
1. **Public Liability Insurance**
   - Minimum coverage: $500,000 USD
   - Covers injury to tourists/third parties
   - Valid for Gambian operations

2. **Professional Indemnity Insurance**
   - Covers errors and omissions
   - Protects against negligence claims
   - Minimum $250,000 USD coverage

3. **Vehicle Insurance (if applicable)**
   - Comprehensive coverage for tour vehicles
   - Third-party liability minimum
   - Valid in The Gambia

4. **Marine Insurance (if applicable)**
   - Boat and equipment coverage
   - Passenger liability protection
   - Coast Guard approved

### **For Itaka Partnership - Additional Coverage Needed:**
- **International Tourism Operator Insurance**
- **European Market Coverage:** €2,000,000 minimum
- **Multi-jurisdiction liability protection**
- **Repatriation and emergency coverage**

## **✅ COMPLIANCE AND ONGOING REQUIREMENTS**

### **Annual Requirements:**
- License renewal by December 31st each year
- Updated insurance certificates
- Staff training record updates
- Safety inspection (if required)
- Financial reporting to GTB

### **Operational Standards:**
- Maintain qualified guide certifications
- Follow established safety protocols
- Report incidents to GTB within 24 hours
- Maintain customer complaint log
- Participate in GTB tourism development programs

### **Marketing Compliance:**
- Use official GTB tourism logos (with permission)
- Include license number in marketing materials
- Comply with advertising standards
- Represent Gambian tourism positively
