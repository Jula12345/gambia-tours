# KUNTA KINTEH ISLAND HERITAGE TOUR
*UNESCO World Heritage Site Experience*

## **KUNTA KINTEH ISLAND & JUFFUREH VILLAGE HERITAGE TOUR**
**Duration:** Full Day (8 hours) | **Price:** €95 per person | **Group:** Max 12 guests

### **TOUR OVERVIEW:**
Visit the legendary UNESCO World Heritage Site where Alex Haley's ancestor Kunta Kinteh was captured. Experience authentic African history, explore slave trade ruins, and connect with living heritage in Juffureh village.

### **DETAILED ITINERARY:**

**8:00 AM - Hotel Pickup & Historical Briefing**
- Professional guide with historical expertise
- Educational overview of slave trade history
- Cultural sensitivity preparation

**9:30 AM - Juffureh Village (Kunta Kinteh's Ancestral Home)**
- Meet descendants of Kunta Kinteh's family
- Traditional Mandinka compound tour
- Oral history and cultural storytelling
- Traditional griot (historian) performance

**11:00 AM - Albreda Colonial Ruins**
- French slave trading post remains
- Archaeological site exploration
- Colonial period education
- Historical artifact viewing

**12:00 PM - Boat Journey to Kunta Kinteh Island**
- Traditional boat with safety equipment
- River historical commentary
- Scenic photography opportunities

**12:30 PM - Kunta Kinteh Island (UNESCO World Heritage Site)**
- Fort James ruins exploration
- Slave holding dungeons and facilities
- Historical interpretation of slave trade operations
- Memorial and reflection time

**2:30 PM - Traditional Lunch**
- Local restaurant with historical significance
- Traditional Gambian cuisine
- Cultural exchange with local community

**3:30 PM - Slavery Museum & Cultural Center**
- Comprehensive exhibits and artifacts
- Educational displays and documentaries
- Contemporary diaspora connections
- Cultural preservation initiatives

**4:30 PM - Return Journey**
- Reflective discussion with guide
- Cultural synthesis and Q&A
- Resource materials provided

**5:30 PM - Return to Hotel**
