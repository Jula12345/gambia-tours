Wildlife and culture loop

For travelers who want a fuller Senegambia route, not only the coast.

This private itinerary combines the Gambian coast, Kotu wetlands, Bakau, Banjul, ferry or north bank logistics, Senegal nature or wildlife stops, village culture, crafts and river or mangrove landscapes.

The package includes private guide/driver, vehicle, fuel, accommodation, airport transfers, border assistance, listed visits and route planning. International flights, meals, drinks, personal expenses, insurance and tips are not included unless confirmed in your quote.

Day-by-day plan

Seven days connecting Gambia's coast with Senegal's nature and culture.

The exact routing is confirmed around dates, border timing, reserve availability and group pace.

Day 1

Arrival transfer and private route briefing

Meet your guide, transfer to the coast and review the route, pace and practical border details.

Airport or hotel pickup included.
Day 2

Kotu wetlands, Bakau, markets and sunset

Explore accessible nature and coastal culture with time for birdlife, local markets and a sunset stop.

Gentle nature and culture day.
Day 3

Banjul and route toward Senegal

Visit Banjul, handle ferry or north bank logistics and continue toward the Senegal route.

Logistics are managed by your guide.
Day 4

Senegal wildlife or nature reserve day

Spend the day around a Senegal nature or wildlife stop with flexible time for photos and observation.

Reserve choice depends on final routing.
Day 5

Village culture, crafts and river scenery

Explore Senegal village culture, crafts and river or mangrove landscapes at a private pace.

Local culture and landscape focus.
Day 6

Return to The Gambia

Return with stops for local life, viewpoints, shopping or photography depending on the day.

Flexible return day.
Day 7

Relaxed final morning and transfer

Enjoy a calm final morning, custom stop if time allows and airport or hotel transfer.

Timing depends on your onward plans.
