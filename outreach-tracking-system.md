## 🎯 RESPONSE CATEGORIZATION SYSTEM

### **🟢 HOT LEADS (Immediate Interest):**
- Response: Enthusiastic, asks for meeting/call
- Action: Schedule meeting within 48 hours
- Follow-up: Send detailed partnership pack
- Timeline: Partnership agreement within 2 weeks

### **🟡 WARM LEADS (Interested but Needs Info):**
- Response: Interested, asks questions, requests materials
- Action: Send comprehensive information packet
- Follow-up: Call within 1 week  
- Timeline: Meeting scheduled within 2-3 weeks

### **🟠 COOL LEADS (Polite Interest):**
- Response: Thanks for info, will consider, busy timing
- Action: Add to nurture sequence
- Follow-up: Contact in 3-4 weeks
- Timeline: Long-term relationship building

### **🔴 COLD LEADS (Not Interested):**
- Response: Not interested, wrong fit, no response
- Action: Archive, try again in 6 months
- Follow-up: Mark for future campaigns
- Timeline: Re-approach different season
