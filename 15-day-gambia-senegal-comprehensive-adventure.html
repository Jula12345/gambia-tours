🎒 PRACTICAL TRAVEL INFORMATION

💳 MONEY & PAYMENT METHODS

💡 Important: Most hotels, restaurants, and local shops in Gambia do not accept credit cards.

🏧 ATMs Available: You can withdraw money from ATMs in major towns (Banjul, Georgetown, etc.)

💱 Best Practice: Bring US Dollars or Euros to exchange for Gambian Dalasi

🇬🇲 Gambian Dalasi (GMD): Local currency for markets, tips, small purchases

🇸🇳 West African CFA Franc: Used in Senegal

💰 Daily Budget: €15-25 for meals, €5-10 for souvenirs/tips

🌡️ TROPICAL CLIMATE & PACKING GUIDE

🌤️ Climate Information:

Temperature: 25-35°C (77-95°F) year-round

Humidity: High tropical humidity

Dry Season (Nov-Apr): Little rainfall, cooler evenings

Green Season (May-Oct): Occasional tropical showers

👕 Recommended Clothing:

✅ Light, breathable cotton clothing

✅ Long-sleeve shirts for mosquito protection

✅ Comfortable hiking boots + sandals

✅ Hat, sunglasses, strong sunscreen (SPF 50+)

✅ Light rain jacket (green season)
