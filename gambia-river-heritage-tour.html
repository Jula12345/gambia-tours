River landscapes

Follow the River Gambia at a calm private pace.

The Gambia River is one of the most important natural and cultural threads of the country. This tour gives you time for river views, small communities, craft or village stops and heritage conversations without trying to squeeze too much into one day.

Optional boat time can be discussed before confirmation, depending on route, season, timing and local conditions.

Suggested flow

A river day with flexible cultural stops.

Exact timing depends on pickup area, river conditions and whether you add boat time.

Stop 1

Hotel pickup and river route

Start from the coast or Banjul area and head toward the river route with context from your guide.

Pickup from Kololi, Kotu, Senegambia, Banjul and nearby areas.
Stop 2

River viewpoints and photo time

Pause for scenery, riverside life and photography. The pace is intentionally slower than a city tour.

Good for relaxed travelers and photographers.
Stop 3

Village, craft or heritage stop

Add a local stop for culture, crafts, history or everyday life depending on route and timing.

Stops are confirmed before the tour.
Stop 4

Optional boat element and return

When arranged in advance, add boat time before returning to the coast or your hotel.

Boat costs depend on route and availability.
