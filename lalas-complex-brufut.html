Lala’s Complex offers modern apartments in Brufut, with a shared swimming pool, free Wi-Fi and air conditioning. With studios and one-, two-, three- and four-bedroom options, you can choose the right amount of space for a short break or a longer stay.

We recommend it as an accommodation option for guests planning Gambia tours with us. The property advertises secure, comfortable accommodation; our team can help you check the apartment setup, arrival arrangements and your individual needs before you confirm.
