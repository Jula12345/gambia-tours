Lamin Conteh · local guide

Local knowledge. A personal welcome.

Born in The Gambia and based in Poland for many years, Lamin brings local knowledge and an understanding of what visitors need to feel comfortable exploring somewhere new.

With more than 16 years of experience organizing tours, he arranges coastal excursions, river journeys, wildlife visits and multi-day routes in The Gambia and Senegal.

GambianTour.com welcomes travelers from around the world with a personal approach, English-speaking guides and Polish guiding available by arrangement.

Tell us what interests you, how much time you have and how you like to travel. We’ll help shape a day or a longer journey around those details.
