Day 1
Arrival in Banjul

Welcome to West Africa! Upon arrival at Banjul International Airport, our team will greet you and transfer you to your beachfront accommodation. Enjoy your first evening with a traditional welcome dinner and orientation about your upcoming adventure.

Accommodation: 4-star beachfront hotel
Meals: Welcome dinner included
Activities: Airport transfer, orientation
Day 2
Banjul City & Abuko Nature Reserve

Explore Banjul's vibrant markets and colonial architecture before heading to Abuko Nature Reserve. This 105-hectare park showcases diverse ecosystems and is perfect for your first wildlife encounters in West Africa.

Wildlife Spotting: Monkeys, antelopes, 250+ bird species
Distance: 25km from Banjul
Duration: Full day experience
Day 3
River Adventure

Embark on a scenic river cruise through pristine mangrove channels. Watch for hippos, crocodiles, and countless bird species while learning about the river ecosystem from our naturalist guides.

Highlights: Hippo pools, crocodile spotting, mangrove exploration
Boat Type: Specialized wildlife viewing vessel
Best Time: Early morning for optimal wildlife activity
Day 4
Historical Journey to James Island

Visit the UNESCO World Heritage Site of James Island (Kunta Kinteh Island) and explore the historical significance of this important cultural landmark. Experience traditional village life in nearby communities.

UNESCO Site: James Island historical tour
Cultural Experience: Traditional village visits
Historical Context: West African heritage exploration
Day 5
Wilderness Trekking

Adventure into pristine wilderness areas for guided nature walks. Experience untouched landscapes and encounter wildlife in their natural environment with our experienced local guides.

Trekking Distance: 8-12 kilometers
Difficulty: Moderate level
Accommodation: Eco-lodge in wilderness setting
Days 6-7
Extended Wildlife Safari

Two full days of intensive wildlife viewing across different ecosystems. From savannah to wetlands, experience the incredible biodiversity that makes West Africa a premier wildlife destination.

Ecosystems: Savannah, wetlands, forest areas
Wildlife Focus: Large mammals, endemic bird species
Photography: Professional guidance for wildlife photography
Day 8
Border Crossing to Senegal

Cross into Senegal and begin exploring this fascinating country. Experience the cultural transition and enjoy your first evening in Senegalese hospitality with traditional music and cuisine.

Border Procedures: Handled by our team
Cultural Transition: Welcome ceremony in Senegal
Evening: Traditional Senegalese entertainment
Days 9-10
Senegal Cultural Immersion

Deep dive into Senegalese culture with visits to traditional craft workshops, local markets, and cultural centers. Learn about local traditions, music, and artisanal techniques passed down through generations.

Workshops: Traditional crafts, textile weaving
Markets: Local artisan markets
Music: Traditional Senegalese music sessions
Days 11-12
Senegal Wildlife & Nature

Explore Senegal's natural wonders including protected areas and unique ecosystems. Experience different landscapes and wildlife compared to your time in Gambia, showcasing the region's incredible diversity.

Protected Areas: National parks and reserves
Unique Species: Senegal-endemic wildlife
Landscapes: Different ecosystems from Gambia experience
Day 13
Final Adventures & Reflection

Your final full day combines any missed experiences with time for reflection and souvenir shopping. Enjoy a farewell dinner celebrating your West African adventure with fellow travelers and guides.

Final Activities: Customized based on group preferences
Shopping: Authentic local crafts and souvenirs
Farewell: Celebration dinner with cultural entertainment
Day 14
Departure

After breakfast, transfer to the airport for your departure. Take with you unforgettable memories of authentic West African experiences, new friendships, and a deeper understanding of this remarkable region.

Transfer: Hotel to airport
Departure Support: Full assistance with check-in procedures
Memories: Digital photo collection provided
