## 📅 **DETAILED DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY**

### **DAY 1: ARRIVAL & RIVER EXPEDITION LAUNCH**
**Location:** Banjul Airport → Kunta Kinteh Island → Georgetown

#### **Morning (Arrival):**
- **06:00** - Arrival at Banjul International Airport
- **07:00** - Meet your Polish-speaking guide
- **07:30** - Traditional Gambian breakfast at riverside restaurant
- **08:30** - Equipment check and expedition briefing
- **09:00** - Transfer to Kunta Kinteh Island (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

#### **Active Adventure:**
- **10:00-12:00** - **BOAT EXPEDITION**: Traditional wooden boat journey to historic slave island
- **Activity:** Navigate Gambia River, learn traditional navigation techniques
- **Cultural Element:** History of trans-Atlantic slave trade with local storyteller
- **Photography:** Colonial ruins and river landscapes

#### **Afternoon:**
- **12:30** - Riverside lunch with fresh fish caught by local fishermen
- **14:00** - **TREKKING**: Island exploration on foot (2 hours)
- **Activity:** Walk historic paths, visit colonial ruins, meet local guides
- **16:00** - **BOAT EXPEDITION**: Continue upriver to Georgetown
- **Activity:** Help navigate, learn river reading skills from local captain

#### **Evening:**
- **18:00** - Arrival in Georgetown, check into eco-lodge
- **19:30** - Welcome dinner with local family
- **21:00** - Traditional **drumming workshop** - learn basic rhythms
- **22:30** - Rest and preparation for tomorrow's village trek

**Physical Activity:** 4 hours (boat expedition, island trekking)  
**Accommodation:** Georgetown Eco-Lodge  
**Meals:** Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner  

---

### **DAY 2: VILLAGE-TO-VILLAGE TREKKING EXPEDITION**
**Location:** Georgetown → Wassu → Lamin Koto

#### **Early Morning Adventure:**
- **06:00** - Wake-up call with traditional Gambian tea
- **06:30** - Light breakfast and expedition pack preparation
- **07:00** - **TREKKING START**: Georgetown to Wassu Stone Circles (8km trek)
- **Activity:** Cross-country hiking through savanna landscape
- **Challenge:** Navigation using traditional landmarks

#### **Morning Expedition:**
- **09:30** - Arrival at **Wassu Stone Circles** (UNESCO Site)
- **10:00-11:30** - **ARCHAEOLOGICAL EXPLORATION**: Ancient megaliths dating 1,200 years
- **Activity:** Photo expedition and historical exploration
- **Cultural Learning:** Local legends and archaeological significance

#### **Active Afternoon:**
- **12:00** - Traditional lunch in Wassu village with local family
- **13:30** - **VILLAGE TREKKING**: Wassu to Lamin Koto (6km)
- **Activity:** Rural village-to-village hiking with cultural stops
- **15:00** - **BIKE RIDING**: Explore surrounding villages by bicycle (2 hours)
- **Activity:** Visit local markets, schools, and craftsmen workshops

#### **Cultural Immersion:**
- **17:30** - Arrival in Lamin Koto village
- **18:00** - **CULTURAL WORKSHOP**: Traditional pottery making with village women
- **19:30** - Community dinner cooked over open fire
- **21:00** - **STORYTELLING EVENING**: Local griots (traditional storytellers)
- **22:00** - Sleep in traditional village huts (authentic experience)

**Physical Activity:** 6 hours (14km trekking + 2 hours biking)  
**Accommodation:** Traditional village huts  
**Meals:** Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner  
**Special Experience:** Sleep in authentic African village

### **DAY 3: RIVER CROSSING & WILDLIFE EXPEDITION**
**Location:** Lamin Koto → River Gambia National Park → Baboon Islands

#### **Adventure Start:**
- **05:30** - Dawn departure with village farewell ceremony
- **06:00** - **RIVER CROSSING EXPEDITION**: Traditional canoe to Baboon Islands
- **Activity:** Learn traditional paddling techniques, navigate river channels
- **Wildlife Viewing:** Early morning bird life and river animals

#### **Wildlife Adventure:**
- **08:00** - Arrival at **River Gambia National Park**
- **08:30-12:00** - **WILDLIFE TREKKING**: Baboon Islands exploration
- **Activity:** Track chimpanzees and baboons on foot
- **Adventure Element:** Jungle trekking with local park guides
- **Photography:** Primates, tropical birds, river landscapes

#### **Active Afternoon:**
- **12:30** - Bush lunch in national park
- **14:00** - **MANGROVE EXPEDITION**: Kayaking through mangrove channels
- **Activity:** Navigate narrow waterways, spot crocodiles and birds
- **16:00** - **FISHING EXPEDITION**: Learn traditional fishing with local fishermen
- **Activity:** Cast nets, use traditional fishing methods

#### **Evening Adventure:**
- **18:00** - **SUNSET BOAT EXPEDITION**: Return journey with sunset over river
- **19:30** - Arrival at Tendaba Camp
- **20:00** - Expedition dinner with fresh fish catch
- **21:30** - **NIGHT WILDLIFE EXPEDITION**: Spotlight tour for nocturnal animals
- **Activity:** Search for hippos, crocodiles, and night birds

**Physical Activity:** 7 hours (trekking, kayaking, boat expeditions)  
**Accommodation:** Tendaba Safari Lodge  
**Meals:** Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner  
**Wildlife:** Chimpanzees, baboons, crocodiles, 50+ bird species
